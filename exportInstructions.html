How to export your data from "My Calendar"

Please note before starting that this page only converts .txt files. My Calendar offers data in a .pc format when prompted to (through backup requests, etc) which is proprietary and encrypted, although there is a workaround way to obtain a .txt file described below. Data files in the .txt format change depending on the user language. This website only allows conversions from English. If you use a different language, do not worry, as the user language can be changed very easily and automatically affects the export files.

How to change user language to English

  1. Open the app or navigate to the main page.
  2. On the top left corner, click on the settings icon (looks like a gear).
  3. Scroll down a little bit until you find the "Language" section. It should have an icon with a chinese symbol and a latin A letter.
  4. Click on this section and select "English" from the list of options.

How to export your data as a .txt file

Please note that the only currently available method will require sending your data through Google Mail or another mail provider. These mail providers have their own user privacy agreements and may use attached documents (in this case your personal information), for advertisement or AI training. Since no other method to export My Calendar data has been found, consider whether the privacy concessions related to this method makes sense for your case before continuing.

  1. Open the app settings (gear icon on the top left of the main screen).
  2. Scroll down until you see "Export document to Doctor", and click it.
  3. It will prompt you to choose a mail provider (like Gmail). Choose your preference. It is recommended to avoid the "chat" options.
  4. An email with your data in .txt format attached will be created. Mail it to yourself.
  5. Congrats! Now you have access to your data in a readable format in both your phone and computer and can procceed to uploading it into this page to receive the equivalent .csv Drip file.
